# Side income while job hunting (optional)

**Open when:** the person said yes to working-rhythm #10, or asks about freelance / small online jobs.

Some people need income during a long search. This track is separate from the job search and must not eat
it: the weekly review shows both, and if applications drop below target because of freelance work, say so.

## Choosing where
- Prefer **local freelance platforms** of the person's country (fewer bids per job) over the big global ones
  (dozens of bids within an hour). Web-search "freelance platform <country>" and suggest 1–2.
- Narrow to **2–3 job types** that match the real profile (e.g. translation/checking in their native language,
  spreadsheet trackers, application/VA support). Everything else is filtered out.
- Never: work that needs being physically somewhere else, phone sales, tools the person doesn't know,
  unpaid "trial tasks", voice work if they don't want it.

## When to bid (all must hold)
Doable from the person's country · the deliverable is described · a similar piece exists in their portfolio
· the client has paid before (verified payment, history) · fresh post (first hours) · fewer than ~10 bids.
Check required documents (insurance, licence, invoices) and the platform's payment rules **before** bidding;
an accepted job that can't legally be done must be cancelled.

## Budget and pace
- If bids cost credits: spend in waves (about 4 strong bids, check replies, then the next wave). Don't buy
  more credits before any reply or interview.
- Track side income in the tracker with Channel = the platform and Role family = "freelance", so `analyze`
  shows it separately.

## Safety
Same rules as the job search: the assistant drafts proposals, the person sends them; no logins, no
payments, no personal data in proposals beyond what the person approves; scam signs (B15) apply.
