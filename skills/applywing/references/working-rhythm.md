# Working rhythm: how often, how much, when to speak up

**Open when:** setup Part 3 (ask these once, after alerts), when the person wants to change the pace, and
when the weekly review shows the pace has dropped.

These came from a real two-month job search run with this assistant. They are **defaults to offer**, not
rules: ask, explain in one sentence why it matters, write the answers into `profile.md` → "Work rhythm".

## Questions (one or two per message, with the default in brackets)

| # | Ask | Default | Why (say it in one sentence) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How often should I look for new jobs automatically? Every day / every 2–3 days / weekly / only when you ask | every weekday morning | New ads fill fast: applying within 72 hours of posting gets more replies. Each search uses some of your Claude usage limit, so more often costs more |
| 2 | Quiet hours: when should I never notify you? | 22:00–08:00 | So the watch doesn't wake you |
| 3 | When should I notify you? Only when you must act / a short daily summary too | only when you must act | Fewer pings, nothing important missed |
| 4 | Which day for the weekly review? | Monday 09:00 | One look a week at what works and what doesn't |
| 5 | How many applications per week is realistic for you? | 8–12 (12–15 if job hunting full time; if Part 1 #9 was skipped, offer both without asking why) | Pace drops silently; the weekly review watches it |
| 6 | Follow-ups and new contacts per week (recruiters, people at target companies)? | 5 follow-ups, 10–30 contacts | Referrals and recruiter messages get past automatic filters |
| 7 | How many applications should I prepare before you approve them together? | batches of 5 | You check a few at a time instead of every single one |
| 8 | Thank-you email after interviews? | yes | Short, professional; I show it and send it after your OK |
| 9 | Are you learning a language that many ads ask for? Which level now? | — | I re-check in 2–3 months and reopen ads that ask for a basic level |
| 10 | Do you also want small freelance jobs as side income while you search? | no | Only if yes: `side-income.md` |

## House rules (explain once, the person may change them)

- **Mix:** about 40% roles where the person has a niche advantage (a language, a rare combination), 40% core
  roles, at most 20% stretch roles, and stretch only when the ad says training is provided and no field
  experience is mandatory. People changing field define the mix in the new field (`segments.md`).
- **Cap per employer:** at most 2 active applications per employer in 30 days, both in the same role family;
  then wait for results. The same CV sent 4–8 times to one recruiting team looks unfocused.
- **No bursts:** spread applications over the week; dozens in one evening look like mass applying.
- **Fresh first:** ads from the last 72 hours go to the top of the list.
- **What to count:** applications per week until the target is reached, then **first interviews per
  week** is the number that matters.
- **Don't judge too early:** replies take 1–3 weeks, a first invite can take about 4 weeks. Change strategy
  after 2–3 weeks of data, not after 3 days.

**No scheduler in this Claude app?** Ask only #1 (used as a reminder at every "continue"), #4 (review day),
and #5–#10; skip quiet hours and notification style (#2, #3), they only matter for the watch.

## Using the answers

- Watch schedule (`watch.md`) uses #1–#3; without a scheduler, on "continue" check whether a hunt is due
  ("It's been 3 days since the last search; shall I look now?").
- Weekly review (`review-and-learning.md`) uses #4–#6 and the house rules.
- Batch approval (`SKILL.md` §6) uses #7.
- Language re-check (#9): write the date in Status; on that date ask for the new level and re-run the hunt
  without that language filter if the level now fits basic requirements.
