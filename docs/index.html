Sık sorulanlar

Ücretli mi?

Asistan ücretsiz. Claude Code gerekir (ücretli Claude planı: Pro ya da Max).

CV'mi siz görüyor musunuz?

Hayır. Dosyaların kendi bilgisayarında, konuşmaların senin Claude hesabında. Asistanı yapan kimse hiçbir şey görmez.

Benim yerime başvurur mu?

Neredeyse: CV'yi ve ön yazıyı hazırlar, formu doldurur, neyi nereye yazdığını gösterir; sen "gönder" deyince gönderir. Siteye giriş ve şifre senin.

İş buldurur mu?

Garanti yok. Zaman kazandırır, uymayan ilanları eler, CV'ne uydurma bir şey yazmaz, mülakata hazırlar.

LinkedIn hesabıma girer mi?

Hayır. LinkedIn otomatik kullanımı yasaklıyor. Asistan sitelerin kendi iş bildirim maillerini ve herkese açık ilanları kullanır.

Telefonda çalışır mı?

Asistan bilgisayardaki Claude Code'da çalışır. Hazırladığı CV'leri telefona atıp oradan başvurabilirsin; istersen her sabah kendiliğinden ilan arar, yalnız bir şey yapman gerektiğinde haber verir.

Bilgilerim nerede duruyor?

Kendi bilgisayarındaki "İş Arama" klasöründe: profilin, CV'lerin, başvuru tablon. Claude seninle çalışırken bu dosyaları okur; başvurduğun sitelere yalnız o başvuru için onayladığın bilgiler gider. Klasör OneDrive gibi bir bulut klasöründeyse oraya da kopyalanır.

Claude kullanım hakkımı çok harcar mı?

Claude planlarının bir kullanım sınırı var; birkaç saatte bir, bir de haftalık yenilenir. En çok ilan ve mail taraması harcar. Asistan bu yüzden taramayı daha hafif bir Claude modeliyle yapar, CV ve ön yazı için güçlü modeli kullanır. Otomatik kontrolü ne kadar sık açarsan o kadar çok harcar. Sınır dolarsa Claude yenilenene kadar bekler; hiçbir şey kaybolmaz.

Your free AI job application buddy, for whichever country you're job hunting in

It reads your CV, searches the job sites you choose (and suggests good ones for your country), filters out ads that would reject you anyway, and writes an honest CV and cover letter for each job, following that country's customs, and fills in the application form. Nothing is sent without your OK.

Install in one sentence: open Claude Code and paste:
Install this: https://github.com/applywing/applywing
Allow the two permission requests, then write "I'm looking for a job" in a new session · getting to know you takes 30–40 minutes (can be split; the more you tell it, the better, and you can skip any question) · Needs Claude Code (paid Claude plan: Pro or Max).

What it does

Gets to know youReads your CV, asks your goals and where you want to work. Your data stays in the job folder on your own computer.
Finds jobsReads alerts from the sites you chose and searches the web. Language, permit, city and scam ads are filtered out.
Writes honest CVsIn the ad's language and the country's format (pages, photo, consent line); Word + PDF.
Keeps trackApplication tracker, weekly summary, follow-up drafts, interview prep.

It never

  • sends an application, email or message without your OK (it shows you what it wrote first)
  • asks for your passwords, ID numbers or card details
  • adds software, years or numbers to your CV that you didn't tell it

Setup

  1. Open the Claude desktop app and switch to Code (or run claude in a terminal).
  2. Paste: Install this: https://github.com/applywing/applywing
  3. Claude asks for permission twice; allow both. Done.
  4. Open a new session and write "I'm looking for a job" in your language. It asks the rest: creates your job folder, installs Python for the CV program if needed (with your permission), and asks one question at a time.
Using claude.ai chat without Code? Download the ZIP → Customize → Skills → + → Upload a skill.

If it doesn't work

  • Did you open a new session after installing? (The assistant loads when a session starts.)
  • Did you allow the permissions Claude asked for?
  • Next time, open Claude Code in your job folder and write "continue".
  • Stuck? Tell Claude: "The Applywing install didn't work, can you fix it?"

FAQ

Is it paid?

The assistant is free. Needs Claude Code (paid Claude plan: Pro or Max).

Can you see my CV?

No. Your files are on your own computer and your chats are in your own Claude account. Nobody who made the assistant sees anything.

Will it apply for me?

Almost: it prepares the CV and cover letter, fills in the form and shows you what it wrote; when you say "send", it sends. Logging in and passwords stay with you.

Will it get me a job?

No guarantees. It saves time, filters out unsuitable ads, never invents anything on your CV and prepares you for interviews.

Does it log into my LinkedIn?

No. LinkedIn forbids automation. The assistant uses the sites' own job alert emails and public job pages.

Does it work on my phone?

It runs in Claude Code on your computer. Send the CVs it makes to your phone and apply from there; if you want, it checks for new jobs every morning and only pings you when you need to act.

Where is my data?

In a "Job Search" folder on your own computer: your profile, your CVs, your application tracker. Claude reads these files while you work together; job sites get only what you approve for that application. If the folder is inside a cloud folder like OneDrive, it is copied there too.

Does it use up my Claude usage limit?

Claude plans have a usage limit that resets every few hours, plus a weekly one. Searching job sites and emails uses the most, so the assistant does that with a lighter Claude model and keeps the stronger one for your CV and letters. The more often the automatic check runs, the more it uses. If the limit runs out, Claude waits until it resets; nothing is lost.
